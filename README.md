# ArtNow - AI Graphics Design Studio

ArtNow is a modern, responsive website for an AI-powered graphics design company. Built with HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript, featuring a clean and professional design.

## Features

- Responsive design that works on all devices
- Hero section with call-to-action
- Services showcase with 6 service offerings
- Portfolio gallery with hover effects
- About section with company statistics
- Contact form
- Smooth scrolling navigation
- Mobile-friendly hamburger menu

## Sections

1. **Home** - Eye-catching hero section introducing ArtNow
2. **Services** - 6 key services offered by ArtNow
3. **Portfolio** - Showcase of completed projects
4. **About** - Company information and statistics
5. **Contact** - Contact information and inquiry form

## Customization

### Adding Portfolio Images

1. Add your AI-generated graphics to the `images/` directory
2. Update the image file names in `index.html` portfolio section (portfolio-1.jpg through portfolio-6.jpg)
3. Ensure images are at least 500x500px for best quality

### Updating Contact Information

Edit the contact details in the Contact section of `index.html`:
- Email address
- Phone number
- Physical location

### Modifying Services

Edit the service cards in the Services section with your specific offerings and descriptions.
